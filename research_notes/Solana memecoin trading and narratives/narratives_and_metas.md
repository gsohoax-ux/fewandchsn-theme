## 1. Chronology of major Solana meme metas, 2024-2026 (examples, dates, peak caps)

### Takeaway
Since 2024 the leading meta has roughly followed this order: viral-animal coins (Sep-Nov 2024), AI-agent coins (Oct 2024-Jan 2025), celebrity and political coins (Jan-Feb 2025, ending with the TRUMP/MELANIA/LIBRA crashes), "cult" coins that survived the crash (FARTCOIN, SPX6900, mid-2025), livestream and creator-fee coins on Pump.fun (Sep 2025), and then a weaker, fragmented 2026 with no new $1B memecoin. In each wave one or two coins reached roughly $0.5-2.5B, then fell 75-99% within weeks to months.

## 2. How a narrative starts and spreads (tweets, news, celebrities, KOLs, AI agents, livestreams, TikTok); timing

### Takeaway
There are four common catalysts: (a) a viral internet or news event (a zoo hippo, a euthanized squirrel, a TikTok meme), (b) a single post by a mega-account (Musk, Andreessen's interaction with an AI bot), (c) an "official" launch by a celebrity or politician, and (d) platform mechanics such as Pump.fun livestream fees. Coins appear within minutes of a viral post. Winners usually emerge within hours to days. Peaks for organic memes come days to weeks later (MOODENG about 2 months, PNUT about 2 weeks, CHILLGUY days), while celebrity coins peak within 1-3 days of launch.

### Cited Findings
- Musk-driven speed: a tweet moved CumRocket +400% in 10 minutes — [CoinGape](https://coingape.com/trending/elon-musks-tweet-pumps-cumrocket-price-400-after-kekius-maximus). A Grok name suggestion was tokenized "minutes later" on Pump.fun — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/309111?amp=1). After the BMNTP post (May 2026), copycats appeared "almost instantly" — [Crypto Times](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/05/11/elon-musks-deleted-post-sparks-bmntp-meme-coin-frenzy-on-pump-fun/). Musk's moves often trigger "a flood of copycat tokens" (Gorklon Rust and Kekius Maximus clones) — [BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/elon-musk-meme-coins-casino).
- News-event lag for PNUT: the squirrel was killed Oct 30, Musk posted Nov 3, Binance listed Nov 11, and the token peaked Nov 13-14, 2024. That is about 2 weeks from news to peak — [Bybit](https://www.bybit.com/en/learn/memes/what-is-peanut-the-squirrel-pnut); [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/solana-meme-coin-surge-binance/); [LBank](https://lbanklabs.com/blogs/top-10-trending-memecoins-of-2024). One source says the token rose from about $3M to more than $1B in five days — [DEXTools](https://www.dextools.io/tutorials/what-is-pnut-peanut-the-squirrel-solana-memecoin-guide-2026).
- TikTok origin for CHILLGUY: launched Nov 15, 2024, and reached about $500M "within days" — [KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/articles/all-about-chillguy-the-viral-tiktok-memecoin-surging-over-6-000-to-a-700m-market-cap); [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/markets/just-a-chill-guy-sparks-tiktok-memecoin-craze); [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/292627/chill-guy-tiktok-meme-crypto-millions?amp=1).
- AI-agent origin: Truth Terminal promoted GOAT after a VC (Andreessen) interacted with and funded the bot — [ChainCatcher](https://www.chaincatcher.com/en/article/2148439).
- KOL / private group origin **[FOLKLORE / anecdotal, a single X post]**: "Most memecoins today start in private group chats… filled with guys who have big ports or major influence on the timeline. They pick a funny trend, a viral meme… and" coordinate the launch and pump — [DREY on X, Nov 2025](https://x.com/dreythehussla/status/1990177574551494918).
- KOL front-running (on-chain evidence): Bubblemaps found that one KOL's wallets bought before 61% of his calls, about 20 seconds ahead on average, and typically sold within 3 minutes of posting — [Bubblemaps](https://blog.bubblemaps.io/bubblemaps-investigates-crypto-kol-moves-onchain/). A Coinwire study (via Benzinga, not verified) of 1,567 coins promoted by 377 influencers found that 76% of the influencers had promoted coins that went to near zero. Coins pushed by accounts with more than 200K followers were down 39% after 1 week and 89% after 3 months — [summarized in search; secondary](https://anndy.substack.com/p/the-meme-coin-market-in-2025-trust).
- Academic support for the influencer pattern: crypto-influencer tweets are associated with positive returns at first, followed by significantly negative longer-horizon returns (Merkley et al., Indiana) — [DL News](https://dlnews.com/articles/defi/crypto-influencers-deliver-only-brief-gains-for-investors/).
- Livestream origin: Pump.fun's creator fees (0.3-0.9% of volume) turned streaming into a token-launch engine in September 2025 — [Alea Research](https://alearesearch.substack.com/p/pumpfun-creator-capital-markets-what); [Bankless](https://www.bankless.com/pump-streaming-payday).

### Inferences
- Bot rule: the "viral post → first token" latency is now seconds to minutes. Being first on the token is a race between snipers and bundlers, not a source of edge. The edge is choosing among the copycats within the first 5-60 minutes.
- Bot rule: KOL call alerts should be treated as an exit-liquidity signal unless the bot is in before the call. The evidence says KOLs buy about 20s early and sell within minutes.
- Escalation pattern (weak inference from the PNUT, MOODENG and GOAT cases): a news or viral event, then a mega-account amplifies it (Musk or a VC), then a CEX listing produces the step-change. Each step can be detected separately.

### Gaps
- No quantitative study found of the time from viral post to winning coin to peak across many events. The timings above are case studies.
- No study found on X Communities, Telegram group growth, or TikTok as leading indicators for Solana memes specifically.

## 3. Copycat dynamics: which coin wins on a crowded story; how traders pick the "real" one

### Takeaway
I found no rigorous dataset comparing "first coin wins" with "best ticker wins" with "best community wins." The case evidence suggests the winner is the coin that first gathers concentrated attention and liquidity, often confirmed by an external "canonical" signal: endorsement or interaction from the original subject or author (Truth Terminal → GOAT), a CEX listing (PNUT), or being the coin the community agrees is "official." Copycats on Musk tweets mostly see "much lower trading volumes but similar results," meaning they also dump.

### Cited Findings
- On Musk-name copycats, "numerous copycat tokens… on Solana with much lower trading volumes but similar results" — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/340273/elon-musk-harry-bolz-memecoins).
- In the Eliza agent-token fight (November 2024), multiple tokens claimed the same identity and prices whipsawed as the community argued over which was legitimate — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/327234/eliza-ai-agent-tokens).
- CTO (community takeover): a true CTO keeps the same contract with new coordinators, while a relaunch is a new token. "Most CTO attempts fail," though successful ones can exceed their prior highs. The Michi CTO's success is attributed to low dev profit-taking, a funny meme, and creative community content — [Bitget Web3](https://web3.bitget.com/crypto-news/rise-of-the-cto-coin-when-communities-take-the-reins-of-failed-memecoins); [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/what-is-a-community-takeover-cto-when-a-memecoins-holders-seize-the-wheel/); [Gate Learn](https://www.gate.com/learn/articles/a-detailed-explanation-of-cto-community-take-over-origins-development-and-impact-on-tokens/5048). CTO coordinators are often the original speculators, so a CTO pump can be an exit — [Bull Crypto Signals](https://bullcryptosignals.com/blog/what-is-cto-in-crypto/) **[low-quality source]**.
- Academic: "Predicting the success of new crypto-tokens: the Pump.fun case" (Marino, Naviglio, Tarantelli, Lillo, arXiv:2602.14860, Feb 2026) models graduation probability in real time using only launch-phase observables. The signals mentioned include bonding-curve progress and **early participation of historically successful traders** — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.14860). MemeTrans (arXiv:2602.13480) uses 122 features (trading activity, holder concentration, bundle statistics, time-series dynamics) to flag high-risk launches — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.13480).
- Base rates: about 1.4% all-time Pump.fun graduation (Dune, secondary). It was below 1% from mid-February 2025, peaked at 1.67% in the week of November 2024, was about 0.7-0.8% in July-August 2025, and about 0.26% in mid-June 2026 — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/343742/pump-fun-rapid-decline-graduating-tokens-memecoin-frenzy-fizzles-out); [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/pump-fun-memecoins-are-dying-at-record-rates-less-than-1-survive); [DEXTools](https://www.dextools.io/news/pump-fun-graduation-collapse-solana-fees-2026). A spike to 6.7% after the BOOST mechanism (July 2026) — [The Block](https://theblock.co/news/defi/2026-07-29-pump-fun-token-graduation-rate-jumps-boost-changes-launch-incentives-409815). 42,000 tokens launched in one day, with fewer than 2% reaching a DEX — [Solana Compass](https://solanacompass.com/news/pumpfun-launched-42000-tokens-in-one-day-fewer-than-2-will-ever-reach-a-dex). Bitquery: 79% of Pump.fun tokens traded only on launch day — [KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/meme-coins-still-matter-7-assets-and-launchpads-to-watch-in-october-2026). Solidus Labs: about 98% of Pump.fun tokens show scam signs — [BeInCrypto (via search)](https://beincrypto.com/elon-musk-meme-coins-casino).

### Inferences
- Bot scoring for copycat clusters (inference, not tested):
  1. Cluster new launches by normalized name/ticker/image hash against a live "story" keyword.
  2. Within a cluster, rank by early smart-wallet participation (supported by arXiv:2602.14860), unique buyer count and the slope of bonding-curve progress, and low bundle/holder concentration (MemeTrans features).
  3. Add a bonus for a canonical signal: the original author or subject replying to, quoting, or holding the coin, or a CEX listing.
  4. Penalize late duplicates once a leader has more than about 5-10x the volume of the second-place coin. Attention concentrates, and the volume gap tends to persist (inference from The Block's "much lower volumes" observation).
- **[FOLKLORE]** "The first coin on a story usually wins": common trader wisdom, but snipers and bundlers have made the literal first contract often a rug. Traders talk about the "OG" coin as the first one that got real volume, not the first deployed. No study confirms either version.
- **[FOLKLORE]** "Ticker = the exact word in the tweet wins" (e.g., PNUT, MOODENG and CHILLGUY all match the meme's literal name). This is consistent with the case studies, but untested.

### Gaps
- No systematic data on winner selection among N copycats (first-deployed vs. best ticker vs. KOL-backed). This would need to be built in-house from Pump.fun launch logs plus an X data feed.

## 4. How long metas last, how fast they rotate, signs of exhaustion

### Takeaway
2024 metas lasted weeks to about 3 months (animal season from September to November 2024; AI agents from October 2024 to January 2025). Since the TRUMP/LIBRA shock in early 2025, the evidence suggests rotation measured in hours to days and lower peaks. Exhaustion shows up as falling graduation rates, falling launchpad fees and volume, the meta's leaders down 75-90% while new entrants pile in, and capital moving to a new attention sink (TRUMP draining AI agents; perps draining Pump.fun in June 2026).

### Cited Findings
- Rotation pace has moved from "weeks" to "hours" (WEEX Labs, "Era of Flash Trends") — [Bitget News](https://www.bitget.com/news/detail/12560605111955) **[exchange research; not independently verified; page not fetchable]**. Lifecycles have gone from 2-3 years/months (WIF) to 2-3 hours (LIBRA) **[opinion blog]** — [CryptBull](https://www.cryptbull.net/2026/03/12/from-150b-to-31b-the-brutal-deleveraging-of-the-memecoin-attention-economy/).
- Attention displacement: the AI-agent meta collapsed 75-90% from January to early February 2025, and analysts said TRUMP "sucked liquidity and attention" — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/ai-tokens-down-90-from-highs); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/ecosystems/2025-01-13-ai-agent-tokens-reel-from-a-steep-market-correction-334213).
- Platform-level exhaustion signal: Pump.fun graduations fell below 1% in February-March 2025 as the "memecoin frenzy fizzles out" — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/343742/pump-fun-rapid-decline-graduating-tokens-memecoin-frenzy-fizzles-out). In June 2026, an 80% drop in activity coincided with rotation to perps — [CoinMarketCap](https://coinmarketcap.com/top-stories/6a3319f6e622116c9d28d72b/). Graduations were about 0.26% in mid-June 2026 alongside an 84% fall in Solana fees — [DEXTools](https://www.dextools.io/news/pump-fun-graduation-collapse-solana-fees-2026).
- Livestream meta decay: prices drop after streams, and one coin fell 93% after earning its fees — [BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/the-risks-and-opportunities-of-streaming-tokens/).
- Academic: turnover responds to lagged absolute returns, consistent with attention-feedback trading (memecoins, ScienceDirect, published around November 2026, *Research in International Business and Finance*-style journal; abstract only) — [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0275531926003491). Meme-index returns predict more frequent future rug pulls (BSC 2020-21) — [Sun et al.](https://chuyi-sun.github.io/repo/papers/meme_token.pdf).

### Inferences
- Exhaustion checklist for a bot:
  1. The meta leader is down more than 50% from its ATH while new copycat launches in the meta keep rising. That is supply up and demand down.
  2. The share of meta-tagged launches that graduate is falling.
  3. Global Pump.fun graduation rate and fees are falling.
  4. A competing mega-event appears: a celebrity or political launch, or a major CEX listing elsewhere.
  5. Derivatives are listed on the meta leader. FARTCOIN and SPX got perps in mid-2025; that marks maturity, not early stage.
- Rotation half-life is regime-dependent. A bot should estimate it live, for example as the median hours from a meta's first $1M coin to its leader's ATH over a rolling 30-day window, rather than hard-coding it.

### Gaps
- No measured dataset of meta durations (e.g., Dune category dashboards) was retrieved. The "hours" claims come from exchange research and blogs.

## 5. Measurable early-strength signals

### Takeaway
The best-evidenced signals are (1) abnormal social attention, which predicts same-day and next-day returns, mostly from user-generated rather than official posts; (2) early participation by historically successful wallets plus bonding-curve velocity; and (3) low holder concentration and bundle share. KOL-call and Telegram signals are weak or negative over longer horizons. I found no rigorous evidence for DexScreener boosts, Google Trends, or Telegram member growth.

### Cited Findings
- Twitter abnormal attention is positively related to contemporaneous and next-day crypto returns, and the predictive effect comes mainly from user-generated tweets, not official accounts (Maître, Pugachyov, Weigert, CFR Working Paper 25-02, data 2018-2022) — [CFR Cologne](https://www.cfr-cologne.de/download/workingpaper/cfr-25-02.pdf).
- Musk/Dogecoin event study (Kent, Apr 2019-Jan 2022): meme-containing tweets were followed by -9.43% abnormal returns, and non-meme tweets by +11.41%. Informative tweets moved prices up to 35x more — [Kent](https://media.www.kent.ac.uk/se/29936/KheireddineAdeyemiCryptocurrancyimpactsofTweeting.pdf).
- Influencer tweets: a short-term pop, then significantly negative longer-horizon returns — [DL News](https://dlnews.com/articles/defi/crypto-influencers-deliver-only-brief-gains-for-investors/).
- Pump.fun success prediction: launch-phase observables, including early participation of historically successful traders and bonding-curve level — [arXiv 2602.14860](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.14860v1). Risk features: holder concentration, bundles, trading dynamics (122 features) — [MemeTrans arXiv 2602.13480](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.13480).
- Telegram: one dataset reports that adding Telegram links to Twitter+website raised the dump rate from 12.66% to 27.61% **[single-author Medium, no methodology; weak]** — [Medium](https://medium.com/@rakesh.therani/the-memecoin-trading-encyclopedia-35-concepts-backed-byproduction-data-6745ab79d1c9). Only about 30 of 112 Telegram "call" listings actually published calls — [ICODA](https://icoda.io/blog/what-is-kol-in-crypto/).
- A study of about 35,000 memecoins reportedly found more than 82% of high performers showed manipulation signs **[unnamed study; unverified]** — [MarketInsiders](https://marketinsiders.in/2026/06/01/how-influencers-secretly-dumped-this-coin-on-you/).

### Inferences
Candidate narrative-strength score (rules to backtest, not validated):
- Social velocity: the z-score of mentions per minute for the story keyword versus a 7-day baseline, weighted toward unique non-KOL authors (CFR finding).
- Mega-account touch: Musk, a major VC, or the original subject or author posting about the story. Weight it highly for the first minutes and apply a quick decay (CumRocket moved +400% in 10 minutes).
- Copycat count: N launches matching the story in the first 30 minutes. Many copycats confirm attention but split liquidity. The score should reward the cluster leader's volume share, not raw N.
- Smart-wallet entry: the number of historically profitable wallets in the first N buyers (arXiv evidence).
- Holder health: top-10 holders' share and bundle share below set thresholds (MemeTrans).
- Negative signals: a KOL call after a price run, Telegram-only promotion, celebrity-team issuance, or the meta leader already more than 50% off its ATH.

### Gaps
- No evidence retrieved on DexScreener trending/boost effectiveness, Google Trends lead time, or X Communities / Telegram member growth for Solana memes. These need in-house testing.

## 6. What makes a meme "sticky"

### Takeaway
The evidence here is mostly qualitative. The winners of 2024-2025 shared a few traits: a real-world, emotionally loaded, already-viral subject (a baby hippo, a martyred squirrel, a "chill guy"); a ticker that equals the meme's literal name; a simple single image; and a timely tie-in to a bigger cultural moment (the US election for PNUT). Durable "cult" coins (SPX6900, FARTCOIN) relied instead on absurdist in-group humor and an ongoing community content engine.

### Cited Findings
- PNUT had an emotional backstory (the internet "cried"), a political tie-in (election week), and a Musk amplification — [Transak](https://transak.com/blog/what-is-peanut-the-squirrel-pnut); [Bybit](https://www.bybit.com/en/learn/memes/what-is-peanut-the-squirrel-pnut).
- MOODENG rode an already-viral internet animal ("hippo meme takes over internet") — [Yahoo/CoinDesk](https://sg.finance.yahoo.com/news/crypto-inspired-moo-deng-zooms-081007908.html). It still reacts to real-world events: +17% on the hippo's first birthday (July 2025) — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/362085/moodeng-memecoin-jumps-17-as-beloved-pygmy-hippo-celebrates-first-birthday).
- The Michi CTO's success came from a "hilarious meme" plus community-made viral content and low dev selling — [Bitget Web3](https://web3.bitget.com/crypto-news/rise-of-the-cto-coin-when-communities-take-the-reins-of-failed-memecoins).
- Galaxy Digital (Will Owens) argues memecoins became a cultural and economic force, while most remain speculative and short-lived — [Yahoo Finance / Galaxy](https://sg.finance.yahoo.com/news/memecoins-no-longer-joke-galaxy-191026926.html). Delphi's "Attention Is All You Need" frames memes as attention assets (secondhand) — [DeSpread Research](https://research.despread.io/memecoin/).
- In the Musk tweet study, tweets containing memes had negative abnormal returns, which suggests novelty or humor alone is not durable value — [Kent](https://media.www.kent.ac.uk/se/29936/KheireddineAdeyemiCryptocurrancyimpactsofTweeting.pdf).

### Inferences
- Stickiness features a bot can score:
  1. The subject already trends outside crypto (TikTok, mainstream news, Google).
  2. The ticker is short and is the literal meme word.
  3. One iconic image matching the original.
  4. Emotional or tribal hook: cute, outrage, political.
  5. A recurring real-world hook, such as anniversaries or follow-up news.
- **[FOLKLORE]** "Animals always come back" and "dogs > cats > everything" are heard often. Dog coins do remain the largest Solana memes (WIF, BONK), and animal categories were "rising" again in November 2026 per [dextrading.com](https://dextrading.com/narratives/solana), but this does not show predictive power.

### Gaps
- No quantitative study found linking ticker length, image features, or humor to memecoin survival on Solana.
